# Neve Brava — boneco de neve snowboarder

Prompt usado no ImageGen:

> Character concept sheet for a stylized arcade snowboarding game called “Neve Brava”. Design one comic snowman snowboarder already mounted on a single snowboard, intended to be converted into a clean 3D model with Tripo AI. Full body, centered, three-quarter front view, transparent background. The character must be extremely simple to animate: three chunky rounded snow volumes, no legs, two simple thick branch arms with mitten-like twig tips, tiny boot stubs fixed directly to the board, and a short scarf whose tail can sway. Strong readable silhouette, oversized head, mischievous friendly expression, carrot nose, two dark coal eyes and three coal buttons. Clothing accents: teal knitted beanie, lavender ski goggles pushed over the eyes, coral scarf. Snowboard is broad and short with rounded upturned nose and tail, thick dark outline, coral/mint/cream graphic blocks, clearly visible bindings. Whimsical hand-crafted low-poly/toon design, chunky bevels, flat colors, subtle soft shadows, very little fine detail, no photorealism, no human anatomy, no poles, no text, no logo, no environment. Production-ready character turnaround feeling, appealing from behind during gameplay, Nintendo 64-era arcade energy with modern clean stylized rendering, original character design.

Sugestão de rig mínimo: um osso raiz para corpo/prancha, um pivô para inclinação do tronco, um osso por braço e um osso curto para o cachecol. As manobras podem ser feitas girando o conjunto inteiro; o corpo só precisa comprimir e esticar levemente no pouso.
