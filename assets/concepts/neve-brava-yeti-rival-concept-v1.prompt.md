# Neve Brava — Yeti rival v1

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: 3D game character concept for conversion into a GLB model with Tripo AI.

Design a funny, memorable Yeti snowboard rival for the stylized web game Neve Brava. Create a compact chunky Yeti already standing on a snowboard: broad shoulders, short sturdy legs, oversized hands, big rounded head, friendly competitive expression, two small blunt ivory horns, pale icy-blue fur in simple large clumps, dark teal face and hands, coral-orange ski goggles on the forehead, mustard-yellow scarf, purple bindings, and a turquoise/coral snowboard.

The feet must remain aligned sideways in a natural snowboard stance while the torso and head twist toward the direction of travel. Use a neutral balanced pose with bent knees and no dramatic lean, suitable for minimal animation. Render the entire character and snowboard in a polished comic 3D style with toy-like proportions, clean silhouette, broad matte fur shapes instead of individual strands, and a genuinely transparent background.

Avoid photorealism, horror, long realistic fur, extreme action poses, direct front-facing camera pose, extra limbs, extra boards, poles, weapons, text, logos, watermarks, cropping, or floating.
