# Panoramas por pista

Vale Bravo mantém a arte aprovada. As outras três pistas usam imagens próprias geradas com image_gen integrado (sem CLI). Arquivos em `public/images/scenery/`. Uma esfera e uma textura ativa, carregadas por demanda; troca de pista libera a textura anterior e descarta downloads atrasados. Neblina própria por ambiente. Sem mudanças na física, HUD ou objetos.

## Prompts finais e arquivos

### canion-cristal-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: 360-degree equirectangular skybox for stylized snowboarding game, 2:1 landscape 1774x887 or 2048x1024. Painted animated-film snowy alpine mountains, organic detailed snow ridges, not triangle icons. Full 360 longitude 180 latitude. Horizon halfway down, peaks between 35%-49% height, foothills fade into mist at 55%, bottom half featureless mist. Top third simple sky. Left and right edges join seamlessly, poles uniform. No foreground objects, houses, characters, track, text, UI, watermark. Bright cold daytime glacial canyon environment. Jagged icy blue mountain ridges, turquoise glacial faces, crisp white snow, pale cyan atmospheric mist and clean blue sky with sparse white clouds. No sunset, no giant foreground crystals.
